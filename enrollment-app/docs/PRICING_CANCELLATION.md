## ❌ Cancellation Policy

### Cancellation Window

- **Can cancel**: Up to **24 hours before the course starts**
- **Cannot cancel**: Within 24 hours of course start time

### How to Cancel

**Online Cancellation (24+ hours before):**
1. Login to your student account
2. Go to "My Courses" or "My Enrollments"
3. Click "Cancel Enrollment"
4. Confirmation will be sent via email

**If within 24 hours:**
- Contact the instructor directly
- Cannot be cancelled online

### Important Notes

- Cancellation is **free** (no penalty)
- You are **not charged** for cancelled enrollments
- Cancellations are processed immediately
- The spot becomes available for other students

## FAQ

### Q: What if I miss a class?
**A:** If you don't show up and haven't cancelled, you may still be charged. Contact the instructor if you had an emergency.

### Q: Can I get a refund?
**A:** Payment is due on the day of class. If you cancel in advance (24+ hours), no charge applies. Otherwise, the full course fee is due.

### Q: What about group sessions or workshops?
**A:** Same pricing (£8 per class/session) and cancellation policy applies to all courses, including special workshops.

### Q: Can I reschedule to a different time?
**A:** Yes! Cancel your current enrollment (if within 24h window) and enroll in a different course session.

### Q: What if the course is cancelled by the instructor?
**A:** You will receive an email notification and have the option to join a makeup session at no additional cost, or receive a full refund.
